# Ahiver — product truth

Ahiver is a live local-discovery marketplace in Nigeria (launched 4 Aug 2026, strongest around Ibadan, Oyo State). Shoppers find shops, kitchens and makers near them, sorted by distance, then message the shop, send an order request, or go pick it up. No download: the app is app.ahiver.com. Sellers open a free shop at seller.ahiver.com and get a Business AI on WhatsApp.

- Audience: shoppers on phones (mobile data, outdoors light) and small business owners who live on WhatsApp.
- Honesty rule: no invented numbers, ratings or testimonials. Live counts come from the public store API.
- Brand v3 (founder, 17 Oct 2026): ink #0B0B0C, white, Ahiver Blue #3B55FF; Bricolage Grotesque display, Inter body. No Playfair, no coral, no purple/navy surfaces; the violet→blue gradient appears only in the logo.
